    Install it in Chrome

    1. Open chrome://extensions. Edge and Brave have the same page.
    2. Turn on Developer mode.
    3. Choose Load unpacked and select apps/extension/dist.
    4. Open YouTube, LinkedIn, X, Kick, Twitch, or Reddit and refresh the tab. The list is already filled with GTA 6.

    On YouTube, Home, Search, Subscriptions, Shorts shelves, and the sidebar are filtered. A matching video you open stays covered until you play it. LinkedIn and X hide the matching post. Kick and Twitch hide the matching stream, category, clip, and chat line. Reddit hides the matching post or comment, including a post whose subreddit name matches. The word list stays in this browser.
