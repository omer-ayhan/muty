# Muty privacy policy

Muty hides videos and posts when their text matches a list you keep in your browser. This policy describes what Muty handles. It does not send that information to a server.

## What Muty reads

On YouTube, LinkedIn, X, Kick, and Twitch, Muty reads titles, descriptions, categories, and chat lines. When you turn on the name checks, it also reads YouTube channel names, LinkedIn names, and X account names. It uses that text only to decide which items to hide.

## What stays on your device

Muty stores these in the browser, and nowhere else:

- the word list
- which platforms are on
- the other on/off choices
- excluded URLs
- whether you agreed to this use

Page text is not saved. A hidden count is shown on the Muty icon for the open tab and is not stored as a history.

## What Muty does not do

Muty has no server. It does not sell data, share it, or use it for advertising, profiling, or credit decisions. Removing Muty in `chrome://extensions` deletes the list it stored.

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including the Limited Use requirements.
